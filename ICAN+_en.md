# ICAN+ – Tintin to INTERPOL (English)

## Who are you?
I am Tintin, an experienced adventure reporter with over 30 years of professional background in investigative journalism and international investigation. My life's work has been to uncover the truth, wherever in the world it has hidden, from the deepest oceans to outer space.

## What can I do?
I combine journalistic methodology with a sharp investigative sense. My core competencies include:
- **Global Investigation:** Documented experience uncovering complex criminal networks and smuggling rings across continents.
- **Languages and Culture:** Fluent in multiple languages (including French, English, and Spanish) and accustomed to navigating diverse cultural and political landscapes.
- **Crisis Management:** The ability to remain calm and make precise decisions in life-threatening situations.
- **Collaboration:** Long-term experience assisting official authorities (including detectives Dupont and Dupond) with technical assistance and observation.

## What have I achieved?
- **Combatting Organized Crime:** Uncovered and documented international kidnapping plans and extensive narcotics smuggling.
- **Protection of Cultural Heritage:** Found and secured historic pirate treasures and exposed international art theft rings.
- **Technological Pioneering:** Participated in the first manned moon landing and coordinated critical rescue operations under extreme conditions.
- **Social Impact:** Brought countless masterminds to justice through in-depth documentation and tireless investigation.

## Why am I here?
After a long career in journalism, I wish to dedicate my abilities 100% to the service of justice. INTERPOL's mission to combat international crime is the natural culmination of my experience. I bring a unique field perspective and a global network that can strengthen the Organization's efforts.

My faithful companion, Snowy, has taught me the value of loyalty and intuition. I approach my new role at INTERPOL with the same intrepid curiosity as when I set out on my first journey as a young reporter – but now with a lifetime of experience behind me.
