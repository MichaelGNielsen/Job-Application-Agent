#### About the Position

INTERPOL is seeking a Senior Investigator for our International Complex Crime division. We are looking for a profile capable of navigating the intersection of traditional policing and modern intelligence analysis.

#### Responsibilities

* Lead international operations against cultural heritage smuggling and technological fraud.
* Coordinate efforts between National Central Bureaus (NCB).
* Handle and de-escalate crises in the field – often in remote corners of the globe.
* **Special Focus:** You must be able to manage and coordinate teams with vastly different working methods. We currently have a case in the field where our two most dedicated (yet often clumsy) investigators, **Thomson and Thompson**, require analytical oversight to avoid diplomatic misunderstandings during their "undercover" work.

#### Qualifications

* **Experience:** Minimum 10 years of intensive investigation. Experience traveling globally at short notice (preferably by ship, plane, or even moon rocket) is an advantage.
* **Analytical Skills:** You must be able to see through complex plots that even a dishonest shipowner or a mad scientist would envy.
* **Languages:** English at an expert level. French is an advantage as we are based in Lyon, but knowledge of Syldavian or Bordurian will be considered a unique strength.
* **Personality:** You must possess youthful energy, an unwavering sense of justice, and the ability to always have a faithful companion (two- or four-legged) by your side.

#### We Offer

* A workplace where no two days are the same.
* Access to the latest technologies in cryptography and fingerprint analysis.
* A competitive salary package and ample opportunity for "field studies" in everything from Tibet to South America.
